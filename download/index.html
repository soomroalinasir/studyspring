Get StudySpring on your device

Pick your device below. Your planner is saved privately on that device, with no account needed.

Android phone or tablet

Android app (.apk)

Install StudySpring as a regular Android app.

Download for Android

The Android download is being prepared. Until then, StudySpring installs on Android straight from Chrome: open it, tap ⋮, then Install app.
Open StudySpring

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the downloaded file.
  2. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps (only for this install).
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
iPhone or iPad

Install from Safari

Apple only allows apps from the App Store, so StudySpring installs from Safari instead. It opens full screen, works offline and looks just like an app.

Open StudySpring
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open StudySpring, then the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Windows, Mac or Chromebook

Desktop app

Installs StudySpring as an app with its own window and taskbar or Dock icon. No download file needed.

Open in browser

Installed! Find StudySpring in your Start menu, Launchpad or app list.

  1. Chrome or Edge: open StudySpring and click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  2. Safari on Mac: open StudySpring, then File → Add to Dock.
  3. Firefox: use Chrome or Edge to install, or bookmark StudySpring.
Your planner lives on the device you use. Each phone, computer or browser keeps its own copy. To move data, use Profile → Backup & export in the app.
